# How do you join a CatchTable waitlist in Korea without a Korean phone number, and keep your place?

Research note for the loopnotes.dev article "CatchTable waitlist without a Korean phone number". All evidence below was gathered on 2026-11-11 unless a row says otherwise. Korean blog posts are other people's reports, not our own experience; they are linked with at most a one-line summary.

## Conclusions

1. Without a Korean 010 number there are three ways onto the line (the CATCHTABLE GLOBAL app with an email sign-up, a store tablet running the multilingual waitlist, or a companion's Korean number), and the first two exist only where the restaurant opted in, with just 1 of 50 sampled Korean posts reporting an email option on a tablet, so a reader looks for them first but plans on the companion's number.
2. A remote place is lost at the arrival check that comes before the call (CatchTable's guide: 300 m GPS radius, pushed back if not checked in by the "confirmed" turn, cancelled after 20 minutes), and Korean posts saw the request arrive anywhere from 6th to 30th in line with three cancellations against one push-back, so a reader stays within reach well before their turn and does not count on being moved back.
3. Two posts say only the phone that joined the line can do the arrival check, even with a friend already at the door, so a Korean friend's number is safe only when that friend stays with the reader.
4. Where a store uses an arrival code, the store posts it where you arrive (at the door in 2 of 6 stores, on the entrance device in 1, and 2 accepted GPS only), so a reader refused by GPS looks at the door and the entrance device first, then asks staff.
5. A missing "Join Remote Waitlist" button usually means "not now" (of 5 stores, remote joining opened only above 50 waiting teams, only for dinner, only from noon or only on weekdays, and one never), so a reader checks again at another time or joins on site.
6. No official page, the App Store listing or VISITKOREA says whether or through which channel the Global app sends the arrival-check request, so a reader using Global keeps location access, app notifications and email on.

## Limits

- Naver rows are self-reports from a sample of top results, not a census; the request turn and time limit vary by store and over time because CatchTable sets them automatically.
- One post (Tonshou) says check-in worked within about 1 km; this conflicts with the official 300 m and is not used.
- The arrival-check channel for Global app and multilingual-waitlist users is not documented anywhere we found. We did not contact CatchTable support.
- Tabling 4.29.0 was confirmed from a search summary, not a full page read.
- No measurement on a device: an app's location check cannot be reproduced on this machine.

## 5. Joining steps on Global (read 2026-11-11)

- App Store listing "CATCHTABLE: Book Restaurants" (developer WADcorp, v2.0.3, English plus 19 more languages): "No Korean phone number required", "Join the waitlist remotely and visit without waiting on-site".
